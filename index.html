Integrity starts with cognition

60 million brains are slowly burning out. That is the number of people living with Alzheimer's disease worldwide. There will be 80 million by 2030. This troubles me deeply.

What is to be done?

Preserving one's mind should not be a privilege.

My goal is to restore cognitive integrity to people affected by neurodegenerative disease, and Alzheimer's in particular. This, through three chapters:

  • First, enable everyone to track and understand the evolution of their cognitive abilities, with no barriers to access.
  • Then, equip each individual with the means to address irregularities in those abilities, and where possible, allow for their progressive improvement.
  • Finally, make these means universally available by aligning the interests of individuals with those of capital holders, so as to foster broad adoption.
