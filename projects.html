  • Early Detection of Alzheimer's Disease Using Natural Language Processing - Sorbonne University
    Only 20-50% of Alzheimer's disease cases are diagnosed early. To contribute to a more efficient detection, I built a multimodal (acoustic + linguistic) pipeline using lexical-syntactic, prosodic, and semantic features from the DementiaBank Pitt Corpus. The model reached F1 macro 0.806, with consistent performance across both "Sick" (~0.81) and "Healthy" (~0.79) classifications.
    This model is intended for use in hospitals and NGOs.

  • Financial Impact Prediction of Natural Disasters, ENEDIS
    Storms cost ENEDIS millions annually in unplanned interventions, I wanted to predict financial exposure through historical weather and cost data. I built a regression model on historical weather, past intervention costs, and geographic variables, embedded into a finance/HR dashboard for 1,100 employees. The model reached F1 macro 0.76.
    The model was deployed in production, presented at C-level, and adopted for ongoing regional budget planning.
