  • Early Alzheimer's Detection via Natural Language Processing, BA in Linguistics Dissertation
    Only 54% of Alzheimer's cases are diagnosed in time, I wanted to detect early cognitive decline through spontaneous speech alone. I built a multimodal pipeline combining lexico-syntactic, prosodic, and semantic features on 552 DementiaBank transcriptions, benchmarked against fine-tuned RoBERTa. The model reached F1 macro 0.806, with consistent performance across both "Sick" and "Healthy" classifications.

  • Financial Impact Prediction of Natural Disasters, ENEDIS
    Storms cost ENEDIS millions annually in unplanned interventions, I wanted to predict financial exposure through historical weather and cost data. I built a regression model on historical weather, past intervention costs, and geographic variables, embedded into a finance/HR dashboard for 1,100 employees. The model was deployed in production and presented at CODIR, and adopted for ongoing regional budget planning.
